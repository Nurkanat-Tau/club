# Stage 5 — Real-world launch playbook (4 weeks)

## Organizer onboarding (30 min per organizer, in person)

1. **Before:** send them the link to `/new-club`, or sit with them while they create the club themselves (description in *their* words).
2. **Together (15 min):** they log in on *their* phone, create their next event themselves, copy the invite, post it to their chat + Instagram story.
3. **Agree the rhythm:** fixed day/time for 4 weeks. Put it in "Расписание".
4. **Agree the 3 duties:** (a) event in Club ≥ 3 days before, (b) reminder via the WhatsApp buttons the day before, (c) mark attendance the same evening.
5. **Add them to a founders' WhatsApp group** with you. Weekly 15-min check-in (Sunday evening).

## Founder pitch (1 minute)

> Мы запускаем Club — место, где люди в Шымкенте находят живые сообщества. Начинаем всего с пяти клубов-основателей, и мы хотим, чтобы ваш был одним из них.
> Что вы получаете: страницу клуба, запись в один клик, список идущих с кнопкой WhatsApp для напоминания, отметку посещений и статистику — кто вернулся. Плюс общий запуск в Instagram: пять аудиторий продвигают друг друга.
> Бесплатно навсегда для основателей. Чат и подписчики остаются вашими.
> Что нужно от вас: 4 недели, встреча каждую неделю, отмечать посещения. Попробуем?

## Instagram launch strategy

**Week 0 (teaser, 3 days):** each organizer posts a story: "Скоро в Шымкенте кое-что для тех, кто хочет найти своих людей 👀" + your account tagged.

**Launch day (same day for all 5):**
- **Reel (you):** "Я собрал 5 людей, которые каждую неделю собирают Шымкент вместе" — 5 quick cuts of each organizer, one sentence each, end with the link.
- **Each organizer:** story with link sticker → their club page; post in their chat.
- **Your brother:** reel from the first run with the link in bio.

**Every week:**
- Organizers post the event link 3–4 days before + a reminder story the day before.
- After each event: 1 photo/story with "следующая встреча — ссылка".
- You: one "building Club" story/reel per week (your founder journey = your personal brand content). Real numbers: "12 человек пришли, 5 — впервые".
- **Cross-promo:** each organizer reposts one other club per week ("кто с нами на шахматы в воскресенье?").

**Content hooks that fit this project:**
- "В Шымкенте нечем заняться? Проверил — вот 5 бесплатных клубов"
- "Пришёл один на пробежку к незнакомым людям. Что было дальше"
- "Я английский учитель и открыл бесплатный speaking club. Сколько людей пришло"

## Member onboarding

- Link → club page → join → **immediately** see the chat button. Organizer greets new people in the chat by name within 24h.
- **At the event:** organizer introduces newcomers ("кто первый раз? 👋"), 5-min icebreaker, photo at the end.
- **Within 24h after:** organizer marks attendance; post photo in chat with next event link.
- **Second-visit push:** anyone who came once gets a personal WhatsApp from the organizer before the next event. This single habit drives return rate more than any feature.

## Event structure (template)

1. 10 min — arrival, name tags / introductions, newcomers welcomed
2. Core activity (run / games / speaking topics / hike)
3. 10 min — hang-out, photo
4. Announce the next date + "bring a friend"

## Feedback system

- **In-app:** 1–5 rating + comment on `/me` after each attended event.
- **In person (you, weekly):** ask 3 attendees: *How did you hear about this? What almost stopped you from coming? Would you come next week — what would make it better?* Write answers in a note.
- **Organizers:** Sunday 15-min call: what took time this week, what broke, what they wish Club did.
- **Rule:** a feature request becomes a candidate only when **2+ organizers or 5+ members** ask independently.

## Analytics dashboard

`/admin` (log in with your admin email). Check it every Sunday before the organizer call. The top card gives the automatic signal; the ⚠️ line tells you which organizers forgot to mark attendance.
