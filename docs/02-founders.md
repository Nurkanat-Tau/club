# Stage 2 — Finding the five founders (Shymkent)

**Honesty note.** Web search can find *public* clubs and pages. It can't reliably show engagement, whether a person is still active, or whether they'd say yes. Everything below is a **lead to check**, not a recommendation. Check each lead yourself using the scorecard in `01-validation.md` §7: look at recent posts, real comments, and whether events actually happen.

I deliberately list **organizations and public pages**, not private individuals. Once you find the person who runs a page, talk to them directly.

## Founder 1 — Running / Walking: your brother ✅

- **Community:** weekly runs and walks.
- **Evidence:** existing Instagram audience (your information).
- **Fit:** has distribution, and a weekly rhythm is natural for this activity.
- **Offer:** founding club, free forever, co-branding in the launch, and the first metrics dashboard.
- **Watch out:** his followers are likely friends and fans. Track how many members come from *outside* his circle, and ask new attendees how they heard about the run.

## Founder 2 — English Speaking Club: you ✅ (recommended)

- **Why you:** IELTS 7.5 and real teaching experience. You'll experience the organizer side yourself every week, which is the best product research you can do.
- **Rule:** don't use your employer's center, its students or its brand without explicit permission. Hold it in a café or anti-café.
- **Existing local players:** there's a public Toastmasters page for Shymkent ([instagram.com/shymkent.toastmasters](https://www.instagram.com/shymkent.toastmasters/)). They're a possible partner, not competition: public speaking is different from casual English practice.

## Founder 3 — Chess

**Signals that the scene exists (fact):**

- Shymkent hosted its first open chess cup in October 2024, including a round-robin GM tournament ([Kursiv](https://kz.kursiv.media/2024-10-27/zhnb-shymkentchess/), [Otyrar](https://otyrar.kz/2024/10/shahmatnaya-osen-v-shymkente-mezhdunarodnye-turniry-i-borba-yunyh-talantov/), [chessnews.info](https://chessnews.info/1-yj-otkrytyj-kubok-shymkenta-krugovoj-gm/)).
- Chess schools are listed on [kursy-shymkent.kz](https://kursy-shymkent.kz/course/interest/shahmaty).

**Who to look for:**

- An **adult amateur player or young coach** (chess schools are mostly kids-focused, and their coaches may want an adult evening format)
- **Café or anti-café owners** that already have chess boards
- **University chess clubs** (SKU / Auezov University, and others)

**Evidence to check:** do they run casual adult blitz evenings, or only kids' classes and official tournaments?

**Approach:** "Adults in Shymkent want casual weekly blitz. You bring the expertise, we bring the players and the tools."

**Offer:** founding status, promotion, and a monthly tournament with a small sponsor prize (find a café sponsor).

## Founder 4 — Table tennis

**Signals (fact):** there are active public Instagram pages for table tennis in Shymkent, for example [@rocket.tennis](https://www.instagram.com/rocket.tennis/) and [@sigma.table.tennis.shymkent](https://www.instagram.com/sigma.table.tennis.shymkent/). Venues are also listed on [2GIS](https://2gis.kz/shymkent/search/%D0%9A%D0%BB%D1%83%D0%B1%20%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BE%20%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%81%D0%B0).

**Who to look for:**

- The **coach or manager of a table-tennis hall**. They have a venue and a business reason to fill tables at off-peak hours.

**Why this is a strong fit:** a venue-owner organizer is *reliable*, because the club is revenue for them. It's also your first natural test of **paid events**.

**Approach:** "We'll bring new amateur players to your hall on Tuesday and Friday evenings. You run a newcomer-friendly game night."

**Offer:** free promotion, and new customers for their hall. Later: we handle registration and payments.

## Founder 5 — Hiking / Outdoor (or backup: board games)

**Signals (fact):** hikes around Shymkent are sold and listed publicly. Examples include Aksu canyon hikes on [Sxodim](https://sxodim.com/shymkent/event/pohod-v-kanon-reki-aksu-2), local tour operators such as [Shymtour](https://shymtour.com/), and Sairam-area tours on [mnt.kz](https://mnt.kz/sairamsu).

**Who to look for:**

- An **independent guide** or a small tour operator that already runs group trips
- A student outdoor club

**Evidence to check:** do they run regular group trips, and do they have safety experience (first aid, transport)?

**Why this is a good fit:** the trips are paid, emotional and very Instagrammable.

**Risk:** it's biweekly at best, and weather and liability are real issues. Also, a commercial operator may see Club as a sales channel, which is fine but different from a community.

**Offer:** a free listing, filled seats on beginner trips, and co-created content.

**Backup: board games.** Look for anti-cafés or board-game cafés in Shymkent that already host game nights. It's weekly, indoor, low barrier, and good for meeting people.

## Where to search (60–90 minutes)

1. **Instagram search** for `шымкент` plus a keyword: бег, running, шахматы, теннис, волейбол, поход, настолки, book club, speaking club. Also check geotags for Дендропарк, Ботанический сад, and the big shopping malls.
2. **2GIS:** search for venues (sports halls, anti-cafés, climbing, table tennis). Venue owners want customers.
3. **Telegram:** search for `Шымкент` plus a hobby word, and check local city chats and student chats.
4. **Universities:** student councils and clubs at SKU (Auezov), SKMA, and other local universities. They're dense, young, and hungry for activities.
5. **Event listings:** [Sxodim Shymkent](https://sxodim.com/shymkent) shows who already organizes events publicly.
6. **Ask your brother's audience:** "Who runs the best club in Shymkent? Tag them." This is free lead generation, and it also works as content.

## Competitive landscape (what already exists)

- **Instagram + WhatsApp/Telegram:** the real competitor. Free, and everyone already uses it.
- **Event listings like Sxodim:** discovery of *events*, not membership or recurring communities.
- **Friend-finding apps:** there are Kazakhstani and CIS apps for finding friends by interest ([bluescreen.kz article](https://bluescreen.kz/kazakhstantsy-sozdali-prilozhieniie-dlia-poiska-druziei-po-intieriesam/), [KTO KUDA](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/id1190271561), [InParty](https://inparty.app/tags), [MakaMeets](https://makameets.com/?hl=en)). They focus on individuals and dating-adjacent matching, not organizer-led clubs.
- **Education-center CRMs** (AlfaCRM, UmaiCRM): they manage paying students, not free communities.

**Our angle:** organizer-led, recurring, local clubs, with the organizer tools as the reason organizers stay. Validate this; don't assume it.
